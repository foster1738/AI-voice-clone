🖥️ Every app on this computer

Zoom, Teams, Discord, WhatsApp/Telegram desktop, Skype, Google Meet, OBS, games. Also normal phone calls through Phone Link (Windows + Android/iPhone) or iPhone calls on your Mac.

  1. One-time setup: install VB-Audio Cable (Windows) or BlackHole 2ch (Mac). On Linux the desktop app creates the microphone for you.
  2. Tap the button above and keep VoxMorph running.
  3. In the calling app, set the microphone to CABLE Output / BlackHole 2ch / VoxMorph Microphone. For Phone Link: Settings → Calls → microphone.
📱 Why not inside the phone's own Phone or WhatsApp app?

iOS and Android do not let any app change the microphone of the built-in dialer or of other apps; this protects every phone call from being altered or recorded by third parties. Voice changer call apps work around it the same way VoxMorph does: the call is placed from inside the app (above), so the other person can be on any normal phone.
